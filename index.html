From the mid-hills to your cup

Ace Beans started in a small kitchen in Kathmandu, roasting a kilo at a time because good Nepali coffee was hard to find outside a handful of cafes.

  1. Grown at 1,500–1,750m

    Cherries are hand-picked across Gulmi, Kavre, Palpa and Syangja, where the mid-hill climate slows down ripening and builds sweetness.

  2. Dried and sorted at the farm

    Cooperatives process and dry the beans on raised beds before they ever leave the district — we don't buy from anyone who skips this step.

  3. Roasted in our Lalitpur roastery

    Green beans reach our roastery in Lalitpur, where we roast in small batches, usually under 12kg, two to three days before your order ships.

  4. Delivered across Nepal

    We ship nationwide via courier. Pay by eSewa or Khalti anywhere in Nepal, or cash on delivery inside the Kathmandu valley — with same-day dispatch inside the valley.

Questions people actually ask

Yes — we ship to all major cities and most districts through our courier partners, usually within 2–5 days depending on location.

eSewa, Khalti, bank transfer, or cash on delivery within the Kathmandu valley. All options are shown at checkout.

Can I skip a shipment?

Yes — skip, delay, or cancel from your account with no notice period and no fee.

Whole bean or ground?
